# Curriculum sources

Saved so the curriculum map can check its citations offline (`python3 curriculum/build_map.py`
fails on a section or category that doesn't exist).

- `openstax/*.toc`: chapter and section titles of eight OpenStax textbooks, taken from the
  books' official contents files on openstax.org (archive version 20260604.144757) on
  20 October 2026. OpenStax books are free, peer-reviewed and licensed CC BY 4.0; their
  figures can be adapted with attribution.
  Biology 2e, Chemistry 2e, Anatomy and Physiology 2e, Microbiology, Organic Chemistry,
  University Physics Volumes 1 to 3.
- `mcat-content-categories.txt`: the content category codes and titles (1A to 10A) from the
  AAMC's "What's on the MCAT Exam?" outline (students-residents.aamc.org/media/9261/download),
  downloaded 20 October 2026. Long titles are cut at the end of their first line.
